Who We Are

Premium Cosmetic Manufacturer in Mumbai & Navi Mumbai

Maulie Cosmetics, powered by SRJ Formulation Pvt. Ltd., is a trusted cosmetic and personal care manufacturing company based in Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra. We specialize in private label cosmetic manufacturing, third party manufacturing, skincare formulations, haircare products, hotel amenity kits, and hygiene solutions.


With a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility at Bathija Industries, Kamothe, Navi Mumbai, we serve clients across India and aspire to deliver international quality standards. Our formulations combine scientific precision with natural ingredients to create products your brand can trust.


As a leading cosmetic factory in Mumbai, we offer end-to-end manufacturing — from formulation development and bulk manufacturing to custom packaging and branded delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Manufacturing

Does Maulie Cosmetics offer private label manufacturing in Mumbai? +
Yes, Maulie Cosmetics powered by SRJ Formulation Pvt. Ltd. is a leading private label cosmetic manufacturer in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. We offer end-to-end private label services including custom formulation, bulk manufacturing, packaging design, and branded delivery for skincare, haircare, and hygiene products.
What is the minimum order quantity for cosmetic manufacturing? +
Minimum order quantities vary by product type and formulation. We offer flexible MOQ options for startups and established brands. Contact us via WhatsApp or the inquiry form to discuss your specific requirements and get a customized quote.
Where is the Maulie Cosmetics factory located? +
Our manufacturing factory is located at Bathija Industries, Plot No.205/206, Jawahar Co.op.Ind.Estate, Kamothe, Navi Mumbai 410209. Our corporate head office is at Maulie Cosmetics, Industrial Estate, Plot No. 234 A, Jawahar Co-op Ind, Kamothe, Panvel, Maharashtra 410209.
Do you supply custom hotel amenity kits with logo branding? +
Yes, we are a specialized hotel amenity kit manufacturer and supplier in India. We provide fully customized hospitality kits with your hotel's branding, logo, and colour palette. Products include comb, soap, body wash, body lotion, toothbrush, toothpaste, and more. We supply to hotels, resorts, and hospitality chains across India.
What types of cosmetic products can Maulie Cosmetics manufacture? +
We manufacture a wide range including shampoo, face wash, face serum, body wash, lotion, cream, hair oil, sunscreen lotion, hand wash, scrub, hygiene wash, ointment, body lotion, toilet cleaner, and complete hotel amenity kits. We can also develop custom formulations based on your specific requirements.
