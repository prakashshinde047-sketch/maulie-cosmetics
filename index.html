Who We Are

Premium Cosmetic Manufacturer in Mumbai & Navi Mumbai

Maulie Cosmetics, powered by SRJ Formulation Pvt. Ltd., is a cosmetics and personal care manufacturing company based in Kamothe, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra. We work with brands across India as a private label cosmetics manufacturer and third party cosmetics manufacturer for skincare, haircare, personal care and hygiene products, as well as hotel amenity kits.


With a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility at Bathija Industries, Kamothe, Navi Mumbai, we serve clients across India and aspire to deliver international quality standards. Our formulations combine scientific precision with natural ingredients to create products your brand can trust.


Serving brands in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Pune and across India, we offer end-to-end cosmetic manufacturing — from formulation development and bulk manufacturing to custom packaging and branded delivery.

Manufacturing Services

Private Label & Third Party Cosmetics Manufacturer in India

From a single hero product to a complete beauty range, Maulie Cosmetics manufactures skincare, haircare, personal care and hygiene products for brands, startups, distributors and hotels — formulated, filled and packed at our facility in Kamothe, Navi Mumbai.

01

Private Label Cosmetics Manufacturing

Launch products under your own brand name. We develop or adapt the formulation, fill it into your chosen packaging and deliver ready-to-sell stock — a practical way to start your own cosmetics brand. White label options let you move faster from an existing base formula.

  • Your brand, label & packaging
  • Flexible MOQs for startups
  • Bulk supply for growing brands
02

Third Party & Contract Manufacturing

Outsource production to an experienced third party cosmetics manufacturer. Share your formula or product brief and our team handles batch manufacturing, quality checks, filling and packing, so you can focus on sales and distribution.

  • Contract (OEM) manufacturing to your formula

  • Quality checks before dispatch

Haircare Manufacturing Solutions

How We Work

Our Cosmetic Manufacturing Process

A clear, six-step path from first conversation to finished stock — the same process for a startup's first product and an established brand's range extension.

  1. 01

    Consultation & Brief

    Share your product idea, target customer, packaging preference and quantities. We recommend suitable formats and next steps.

  2. 02

    Formulation & Sampling

    Our team develops a new formula or adapts an existing base, and prepares samples for your review and approval.

  3. 03

    Packaging & Branding

    Select bottles, tubes, jars, pumps and labels. We support you in matching packaging to your brand and price point.

  4. 04

    Bulk Manufacturing

    Approved formulas are produced in batches at our manufacturing facility in Kamothe, Navi Mumbai.

  5. 05

    Quality Testing

    Each batch is checked against the approved standard before it is released for filling.

  6. 06

    Filling, Packing & Dispatch

    Products are filled, sealed, packed and dispatched to your warehouse or customers across India.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cosmetic Manufacturing FAQs

Does Maulie Cosmetics offer private label manufacturing in Mumbai? +
Yes, Maulie Cosmetics powered by SRJ Formulation Pvt. Ltd. is a leading private label cosmetic manufacturer in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. We offer end-to-end private label services including custom formulation, bulk manufacturing, packaging design, and branded delivery for skincare, haircare, and hygiene products.
What is the minimum order quantity for cosmetic manufacturing? +
Minimum order quantities vary by product type and formulation. We offer flexible MOQ options for startups and established brands. Contact us via WhatsApp or the inquiry form to discuss your specific requirements and get a customized quote.
Where is the Maulie Cosmetics factory located? +
Our manufacturing factory is located at Bathija Industries, Plot No.205/206, Jawahar Co.op.Ind.Estate, Kamothe, Navi Mumbai 410209. Our corporate head office is at Maulie Cosmetics, Industrial Estate, Plot No. 234 A, Jawahar Co-op Ind, Kamothe, Panvel, Maharashtra 410209.
Do you supply custom hotel amenity kits with logo branding? +
Yes, we are a specialized hotel amenity kit manufacturer and supplier in India. We provide fully customized hospitality kits with your hotel's branding, logo, and colour palette. Products include comb, soap, body wash, body lotion, toothbrush, toothpaste, and more. We supply to hotels, resorts, and hospitality chains across India.
What types of cosmetic products can Maulie Cosmetics manufacture? +
We manufacture a wide range including shampoo, face wash, face serum, body wash, lotion, cream, hair oil, sunscreen lotion, hand wash, scrub, hygiene wash, ointment, body lotion, toilet cleaner, and complete hotel amenity kits. We can also develop custom formulations based on your specific requirements.
What is the difference between private label and third party cosmetic manufacturing? +
In private label manufacturing, products are made and sold under your brand name, often starting from a ready or customised formula. In third party (contract) manufacturing, we produce products to your own formula or specification. Maulie Cosmetics offers both, along with custom formulation development.
Can Maulie Cosmetics help me start my own cosmetics brand? +
Yes. We work with startups and first-time founders, guiding them through product selection, formulation, packaging and flexible minimum order quantities, and then supporting bulk manufacturing as the brand grows.
Do you develop custom skincare and haircare formulations? +
Yes. We develop custom formulations to your brief — including texture, fragrance, key ingredients and packaging — for skincare, haircare and personal care products. Share your requirements via WhatsApp or the inquiry form to begin.
Which cities and regions do you serve? +
Our factory is in Kamothe, Navi Mumbai (Panvel), Maharashtra. We serve brands in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Panvel, Thane, Pune and across Maharashtra, and dispatch finished products to clients across India.
Do you manufacture home care and hygiene products? +
Yes. Along with cosmetics, we manufacture hygiene and household products such as toilet cleaner, hand wash and intimate hygiene wash, for private label and bulk supply.
How do I get a quote for cosmetic manufacturing? +
Send us your product type, expected quantity and packaging preference by WhatsApp or phone on +91 89768 56389, by email at info@mauliecosmetics.com, or through the inquiry form on this page. Our team will respond with the next steps and a quote.
